## Canonical correction

EchoAuth operates between two footprints:

- the **natural footprint**, preserving lived reality, judgment, action, consequence, meaning, and what actually occurred;
- the **digital footprint**, preserving governing records, repositories, branches, checkpoints, contracts, authority records, validations, acceptance states, and returned evidence.

Neither footprint replaces the other. Neither footprint alone is intent.

> Intent is preserved through correspondence between the natural footprint and the digital footprint.

EchoAuth does not originate intent and must not fish for intent from repository patterns. Its responsibility is to govern legitimate formation, passage, bounded completion, evidence return, acceptance boundaries, WAIT, and STOP so that the two footprints remain truthfully related.

## Native application

- **Adumetric** forms bounded responsibility from the demonstrated correspondence between natural and digital evidence; it does not manufacture intent.
- **Hawk** preserves passage identity and authority while responsibility moves; it does not convert digital readiness into natural permission.
- **Saloherm** completes bounded work and returns digital evidence of natural or represented consequence; it does not turn completion into acceptance or continuation.

## Corrected correspondence test

Before movement, EchoAuth asks:

1. What does the natural footprint demonstrate?
2. What does the digital footprint preserve?
3. Where do they correspond?
4. Where do they diverge?
5. Which responsibility belongs to this divergence or transition?
6. What exact authority permits movement?
7. What evidence must return to test correspondence again?
8. Where is WAIT or STOP?
