## Decision

The N.B.C. Timestamp Provenance Standard applies prospectively to the S-Kernel / Stabilizer / S1-S23 lineage and any future consequential stabilizer- or kernel-related governance record.

This does not retroactively rewrite historical journal entries or pre-standard assessments. Their original dates, text, Git history, and evidentiary status remain preserved.

## Preserved kernel / stabilizer evidence surfaces

The current repository already preserves two important bounded records:

- `docs/assessments/s-kernel-stabilizer-continuity-assessment-20260807.md`
- `docs/assessments/ori-geometric-field-stabilizer-kernel-crossroads-ancestor-map-2026-08-27.md`

The first preserves the current governance boundary: S1-S23 modules may process, classify, stabilize, reason, route, observe, score, or support, but their outputs are not authority. MCG/MPC governs state and permissibility; CEG governs movement sequencing; EchoAuth enforces permission; execution remains bounded.

The second preserves the historical lineage from ORI / geometric-field precursors through Stability Regulation Kernel, specialized stabilizers / S-modules, Safe Kernel integration, crossroads geometry, and CEG ordered release while keeping historical journal language distinct from current executable truth.

## Timestamp application rule

For any consequential stabilizer or kernel record created, amended, corrected, reissued, or used to establish a new decision after the standard became effective, preserve:

- **Timestamp**
- **Event**
- **Status**
- **Authority / Source**
- **Evidence Boundary**
- **Reference / Checkpoint**

Historical records are not re-dated. If an older stabilizer or kernel record becomes consequential again, the new amendment or assessment receives the new semantic timestamp and references the preserved historical checkpoint.

`HISTORICAL KERNEL RECORD TIME != LATER ALIGNMENT TIME`

`TIMESTAMP != ORIGIN OF THOUGHT OR UNDERSTANDING`

## Stabilizer-family boundary

The stabilizer family remains responsibility-separated rather than flattened into one interchangeable stabilizer. A timestamp does not merge S-modules, transfer responsibility between them, create state authority, create permission, activate kernel modes, or authorize execution.

A future record may establish one of those relationships only through independent evidence and the valid governance boundary for that responsibility.

## Evidence boundary

This alignment establishes provenance discipline only. It does not establish:

- current executable ORI geometry;
- implemented S1-S23 kernel runtime;
- universal safety or production readiness;
- runtime activation or kernel-mode activation;
- permission, execution, deployment, trading, or autonomous-action authority;
- ownership, authorship, novelty, priority, patentability, or external validation;
- that historical journal descriptions are identical to present engineering truth.

No prior disposition is changed by this record.
