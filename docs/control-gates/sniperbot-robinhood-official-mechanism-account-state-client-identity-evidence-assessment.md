## Evidence Sending

Authoritative Robinhood evidence establishes:

1. A customer first connects a third-party AI platform to the Robinhood Trading MCP.
2. During authentication, Robinhood prompts the customer to open the dedicated Agentic account.
3. The Agentic account is a self-directed individual investing account.
4. A primary individual investing account in good standing is required before opening the additional Agentic account.
5. Account onboarding and agent authentication must be completed on a desktop device.
6. Robinhood publishes one official Trading MCP endpoint for supported MCP clients.
7. Robinhood lists ChatGPT, Codex, Claude, Cursor, Grok, and other MCP-compatible platforms as supported client categories.
8. Once connected, the agent receives read access to all Robinhood accounts, including account numbers, positions, balances, transactions, order history, watchlists, and scans.
9. Trade placement is restricted to the dedicated Robinhood Agentic account.
10. Robinhood currently describes long equities and options as available trading surfaces for the agentic account.
11. A customer can review proposed activity before action, but Robinhood also warns that an agent may place trades without per-trade confirmation if the customer has instructed it to act that way.
12. The customer remains responsible for all agent activity and can disconnect the agent from the app.
