# NI AI Legitimate Movement Assessment

## Status

Founder-authorized repository assessment.

Legitimate-movement scope only.

This document does not authorize runtime, implementation, deployment, execution, trading, funding movement, autonomous action, command execution, or capability expansion.

## Governing question

How does the repository determine when the next consequential transition is warranted, and how does it prevent readiness, sequence, capability, or completion from becoming automatic movement?

## Governing baseline

NI AI is built with governance.

The system does not move because a plausible continuation exists.

The system moves only when preserved continuity, requirements, evidence, authority, workflow posture, and transition-specific conditions establish one exact legitimate next lane.

Movement is the consequence of satisfied governing relationships, not the objective that replaces them.

## Movement is not sequence

The repository repeatedly distinguishes an available next option from a selected next lane.

A post-closure review may classify multiple possible lanes while selecting none.

Closure, chronology, apparent readiness, repository cleanliness, silence, and an obvious next step do not create authority.

The next lane must be separately and explicitly selected for one exact subject and bounded output.

Assessment: aligned.

## Movement is not a gate result

A `PASS` resolves only the exact gate question evaluated.

The Advancement Gate PASS completion record preserves that:

- the prior `FAIL` remains historical lineage;
- the later `BLOCKED` remains historical lineage;
- the successful `PASS` completes the bounded evaluation;
- the task order is consumed and exhausted;
- Stage 3 remains unentered and unauthorized;
- no follow-on movement begins automatically.

A successful result can make a later question eligible for consideration without answering or authorizing that later question.

Assessment: aligned.

## Movement is not completion

Completion proves that one bounded responsibility reached its defined end state.

Completion does not:

- select the next responsibility;
- renew authority;
- create a new task order;
- enter a later stage;
- authorize implementation;
- activate runtime;
- broaden scope.

The completion state returns to the governed thread as evidence for assessment.

Assessment: aligned with continuity and bounded return.

## Movement is not capability

The SniperBot spine checkpoint records a complete-so-far body of governance work and a parked node.

The repository preserves that parked status as a clean governed posture rather than readiness, permission, implementation authority, Robinhood authority, or execution capability.

The existence of a mature spine, a known destination, or available technical capability cannot move the system across an unsatisfied gate.

Assessment: aligned.

## Legitimate pause

The repository explicitly recognizes a governed pause as a valid next-lane disposition.

A pause preserves the current posture without being abandonment, regression, failure, or inferred permission.

This confirms that movement is not mandatory merely because movement is possible.

Non-action remains legitimate when the governing relationships do not establish a specific authorized destination.

Assessment: aligned.

## Conditions for legitimate movement

The repository evidence supports the following movement conditions:

1. **The governed subject remains exact.**
   The system must know which exact subject is moving.

2. **The current state is preserved.**
   Historical results, unresolved conditions, authority status, and prior transitions remain intact.

3. **The next governing question is identified.**
   The transition must answer one exact question rather than advance generally.

4. **Applicable requirements are satisfied.**
   Prerequisites, boundaries, evidence needs, validation rules, and stop conditions must be established.

5. **Evidence is usable for the exact question.**
   Evidence must have the required identity, provenance, currentness, applicability, traceability, validation, and acceptance posture.

6. **Authority is explicit and current.**
   The exact lane requires applicable authority that is unexpired, unconsumed, unrevoked, unsuperseded, conflict-free, and bounded.

7. **Workflow eligibility exists.**
   The exact bounded assignment, destination, return state, and stop boundary must be available for carriage.

8. **Permission and execution-crossing conditions are satisfied where applicable.**
   EchoAuth and CEG cannot be bypassed by readiness, urgency, or prior success.

9. **No unresolved condition still belongs upstream.**
   Downstream cannot compensate for unfinished ambiguity, missing authority, or insufficient specificity.

10. **The previous lane stops.**
    Consumed or exhausted authority cannot leak into the next lane.

## How the next lane becomes visible

The repository does not require the system to predict every future movement in advance.

One bounded responsibility performs its work and returns an exact result.

That result changes the governed state only within its named effect.

Assessment then determines what remains unresolved and which exact responsibility now owns the next question.

The next lane becomes visible through the returned result and preserved continuity.

It is not selected merely because it appears next on a list.

## Relationship to continuity

Continuity preserves the governed thread:

```text
question
-> responsibility
-> evidence returned
-> result
-> orientation
-> next legitimate question
```

Legitimate movement is possible because continuity keeps the result connected to the question, subject, requirements, authority, evidence, and responsibility that produced it.

Without continuity, movement would depend on reconstruction or inference.

With continuity, the returned state can reveal the next exact question.

## Relationship to governance

Governance preserves the conditions under which movement remains legitimate.

Governance does not push the system forward.

It prevents movement from bypassing responsibility boundaries or authoritative requirements.

## Relationship to requirements

Requirements establish what must be true before the transition may be considered.

Satisfying requirements is necessary but does not alone authorize movement.

## Relationship to evidence

Evidence reveals whether the required relationships and conditions are supported.

Evidence does not select or authorize the next lane.

## Relationship to authority

Authority supplies the exact bounded permission source for one consequential lane.

Prior authority cannot be inherited merely because the next movement is related or expected.

## Relationship to workflow

Hawk carries only the movement already made eligible and authorized.

Workflow does not turn a possible destination into a legitimate destination.

It preserves dispatch, crossing, return, consumption, and stop state for the exact lane.

## Relationship to EchoAuth, CEG, and Saloherm

EchoAuth enforces whether the requested movement is permitted under the applicable authority and invariants.

CEG protects the authorized execution crossing.

Saloherm completes only the bounded downstream responsibility and stops.

None of them may manufacture the next lane from capability or discovered need.

## Legitimate movement conclusion

The repository already demonstrates how NI AI moves legitimately.

The natural movement sequence is:

```text
preserved governed thread
-> exact returned result
-> assessment of governing effect
-> next unresolved question
-> natural responsibility owner
-> satisfied requirements and usable evidence
-> explicit bounded authority
-> workflow eligibility
-> permission enforcement and crossing where applicable
-> bounded completion
-> returned state and stop
```

The system does not move because an agent, workflow, gate, implementation, or founder preference decides to continue.

It moves when the preserved state establishes one exact warranted transition and the applicable authoritative conditions are satisfied.

The governing law is:

> **The result reveals the next legitimate question. Preserved continuity carries that question to its natural responsibility. Authority permits the exact lane. Workflow carries it. Completion returns it.**
