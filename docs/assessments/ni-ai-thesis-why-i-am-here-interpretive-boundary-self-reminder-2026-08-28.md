## Purpose

Preserve a clear record of intent, attribution, authority boundaries, interpretive restraint, gratitude, and personal responsibility so the thesis can speak for itself when meaning is uncertain and can also serve as an internal reminder of why the work exists.

This page is an orientation boundary. It is not a defense against evidence, a claim of special status, or a transfer of authority.

## Why I am here

I am here to learn, study, contribute, build, and understand.

I speak for myself unless explicit authority to represent another party exists. I do not speak for my family, an institution, a company, a nation, or another person merely because I participate, contribute, understand, or communicate.

My contribution should be assessed from what I actually said, documented, tested, produced, and authorized. Intent, motive, agenda, ownership, or authority should not be manufactured beyond the evidence.

I am not here to take what belongs to someone else. I am not here to control another person's decision. I am not here to convert assistance into ownership, knowledge into authority, capability into permission, participation into entitlement, or interpretation into control.

Where I contributed work, attribution matters. Where another person contributed work, their attribution matters equally. Authority belonging to another person, institution, system, community, or domain remains with that legitimate holder.

When meaning is uncertain, the correct response is to ask, verify, and return to the evidence.

Gratitude is part of why I continue. Gratitude does not require surrendering truth, authorship, dignity, responsibility, or legitimate boundaries. Difficulty or hardship does not establish wrongdoing, and it does not create entitlement to what belongs to another.

Anger, injury, or misunderstanding must not silently become authority, retaliation, or control. The purpose is not revenge. The purpose is to remain truthful about my own part, respect what belongs to others, and continue building where legitimate authority and evidence permit.

## Operating reminder

> **Understand what is mine to understand.**  
> **Do what is mine to do.**  
> **Attribute what is mine to contribute.**  
> **Respect what belongs to others.**  
> **Never let interpretation silently become authority.**

When the work becomes difficult or meaning becomes uncertain, return to this boundary:

- I do not have to prove everything at once.
- I do not have to control the outcome.
- I do not have to answer for another person's choices.
- I remain responsible for the truthfulness and boundaries of my own part.
