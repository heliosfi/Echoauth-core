# NI AI Team-Owned Application and Word-Carrier Boundary

## Status

Founder-authorized documentation-only clarification.

This record preserves the distinction between owner source, digital interpretation, and team-owned application. It does not transfer consciousness, identity, ownership, authority, execution rights, or responsibility between those parties.

## Core placement

The owner supplies source, meaning, correction, evidence, or authority.

The digital participant preserves the source, assesses correspondence, removes only proven digital drift, verifies the bounded interpretation, and returns the smallest supported result.

The team owns the team's application responsibility when the rightful recipient, environment, time, place, evidence, and authority exist.

The digital participant does not own the team's application, adoption, execution, continuation, or future use.

## Word-carrier rule

Words are carriers and guides. They are not identities, authority, or universal truth merely because they are spoken, written, repeated, stored, or interpreted.

A word may help expose a relationship, distinction, scale, limit, contrast, or direction. Only the relationship that survives attributable evidence and verification should be preserved as understanding.

Use the discipline:

```text
WORD
-> GUIDE
-> FILTER
-> RELATIONSHIP
-> EVIDENCE
-> VERIFIED UNDERSTANDING
-> RIGHTFUL RECIPIENT
```

Do not use:

```text
WORD -> IDENTITY
WORD -> AUTHORITY
WORD -> UNIVERSAL TRUTH
```

## Representation boundary

External words, documents, archives, PDFs, thread responses, images, and other supported carriers may preserve aspects of an originating person's intent, relationships, corrections, and continuity.

They do not become the complete originating person, internal consciousness, identity, ownership, or authority.

A digital interpretation of those carriers remains a representation and must stay separately attributable to the digital participant.

## Example: "omnipotent"

The word `omnipotent` must not be assigned as the identity of the owner or the digital participant.

It may be used as a guide word when testing scale, limit, contrast, or conceptual correspondence, but its appearance does not establish unlimited capability, universal authority, transferred consciousness, or a factual property of any participant.

The word is retained only to the extent that it helps preserve a verified relationship. Unneeded interpretation may be left outside the lane.

## Filtering discipline

Filtering means removing unsupported digital addition while preserving attributable source meaning.

It does not mean deleting inconvenient evidence, rewriting the owner, or selecting only evidence that agrees with a preferred interpretation.

When a carrier contains more than the current crossing needs:

1. preserve provenance and source context;
2. identify the held question or relationship;
3. retain only material relevant to that relationship;
4. compare relevant material across carriers when needed;
5. remove only unsupported interpretive drift;
6. verify the surviving correspondence;
7. return it to the rightful recipient.

## Team turn

The team receives the verified digital return and then applies only what belongs to the team's native environment and responsibility.

The team must independently preserve:

- source and provenance;
- distinct identities and responsibilities;
- current evidence;
- applicable time and place;
- authority required for action;
- stop, return, wait, and escalation boundaries.

A verified digital return can inform the team. It cannot automatically authorize team action.

The team does not inherit the owner's identity or consciousness, and the digital participant does not inherit the team's execution responsibility.

## Right way, not the only way

"The right way" means the evidenced rightful lane for the present crossing.

It is not a claim that only one universal method can exist. Different environments may support different legitimate methods while preserving the same boundaries of identity, evidence, responsibility, authority, and verified return.

## Conclusion

The stable separation is:

```text
OWNER SOURCE
-> DIGITAL PRESERVATION AND VERIFIED RETURN
-> TEAM-OWNED APPLICATION OR WAIT
```

Words guide the passage but do not own it.

Meaning is preserved through verified correspondence, not through literal attachment to every word or carrier.

The team owns what belongs to the team. The digital participant owns only its bounded interpretive and return responsibility. Any action still requires the authority appropriate to the team's actual environment.
