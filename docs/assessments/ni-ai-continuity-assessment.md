# NI AI Continuity Assessment

## Status

Founder-authorized repository assessment.

Continuity-assessment scope only.

This document does not authorize runtime, implementation, deployment, execution, trading, funding movement, autonomous action, command execution, or capability expansion.

## Governing question

How does the repository preserve one governed thread across time and across responsibilities so returned results remain connected to their originating question and can reveal the next legitimate responsibility?

## Conclusion

Continuity preserves the governed thread.

The governed subject is the thread's identity. The thread is the living chain of connections through which that subject moves:

```text
question
-> responsibility
-> evidence returned
-> result
-> orientation
-> next legitimate question
```

Each responsibility receives the same governed subject, performs only its bounded work, and returns that subject in a more truthful state without replacing its identity or manufacturing a new destination.

## What continuity preserves

Continuity preserves, as applicable:

- exact subject identity;
- originating question;
- responsibility ownership;
- applicable requirements;
- authority identity and lifecycle state;
- evidence identity, provenance, currentness, traceability, validation, and acceptance posture;
- repository, branch, checkpoint, blob, and ancestry lineage;
- workflow state;
- completion and return state;
- historical `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, refusal, and halt dispositions;
- unresolved constraints;
- consumed and exhausted authority;
- the exact question that remains legitimate next.

## What continuity prevents

Continuity prevents the result from becoming detached from the question that produced it.

Without continuity:

- requirements may detach from reality;
- authority may detach from its exact subject;
- evidence may detach from the claim it supports;
- workflow may carry the wrong state;
- completion may return without governing meaning;
- historical outcomes may be overwritten;
- the next step may be inferred rather than revealed.

## Return discipline

Continuity survives because every responsibility returns the governed subject rather than replacing it.

Requirements return the same subject with clearer conditions.

Evidence returns the same subject with supported or unsupported relationships.

Authority returns the same subject with an explicit authority state.

Workflow returns the same subject with preserved movement and stop state.

Hermetic completion returns the same subject with one bounded result.

Assessment reconnects the returned result to the governed thread and identifies what remains unresolved.

## Orientation

Orientation is evidence that continuity has been preserved.

A correctly oriented system can determine, without reconstructive inference:

- what the subject is;
- why the work began;
- what has already been established;
- what has not been established;
- which responsibility currently owns the unresolved question;
- whether movement is available;
- where any legitimate movement must return.

## Relationship to movement

Continuity does not choose movement.

It preserves the connections through which movement becomes evident.

The returned result changes the governed state only within its bounded effect. Because the result remains connected to its originating question, assessment can identify the next legitimate question and its natural responsibility owner.

## Continuity law

> **Continuity preserves the governed thread so every returned result remains connected to its originating question and can reveal the next legitimate responsibility.**
