## Responsibility boundaries

### Natural Intellect

Preserves connection to reality, meaning, context, and the originating governed subject.

It does not authorize movement or perform bounded execution.

### Adumetric

Performs upstream asymmetric work.

It reduces ambiguity, discovers relationships, establishes specificity, identifies requirements, connects evidence, and prepares exact bounded work.

It does not perform downstream completion.

### Requirements

Establish what must be true before movement may be considered.

They do not create evidence, authority, permission, workflow release, or execution.

### Evidence

Reveals whether requirements, claims, states, results, and relationships are supported.

It does not create authority or movement.

### Authority

Supplies the explicit, current, bounded governing source for one named consequential lane.

It is not ambient, inherited, or manufactured by readiness, capability, chronology, evidence, or completion.

### Hawk

Carries the exact bounded workflow state.

It preserves dispatch, handoff, currentness, crossing posture, return, consumption, and stop state.

It does not invent meaning, requirements, or authority.

### EchoAuth

Enforces permission under the applicable authority, identity, policy, invariants, action, resource, payload, time, and runtime envelope.

It does not originate founder authority.

### CEG

Protects the authorized crossing into one bounded execution cycle.

It does not create upstream meaning, authority, or downstream completion.

### Saloherm

Performs only the exact bounded downstream responsibility.

It preserves scope, produces the bounded result, returns it, and stops.

It does not widen scope, infer missing requirements, or authorize adjacent work.

### Assessment

Reconnects the returned result to the governed thread.

It determines the exact governing effect of the result and identifies the next unresolved question.

It does not manufacture a next lane where the result does not reveal one.

## Hermetic-asymmetric integration

The asymmetric and hermetic responsibilities form a governed air lock.

Asymmetric work must reduce ambiguity until downstream completion becomes legitimate.

Hermetic work must preserve exactness and return a bounded result before upstream reconnection.

The relationship is:

> **Adumetric prepares. Hawk carries. EchoAuth enforces permission. CEG protects the crossing. Saloherm completes and stops.**

No responsibility performs another responsibility's work.
