## Purpose belongs to reality

The strongest journal-derived human/system boundary is:

> **Purpose belongs to reality before it belongs to the architecture.**

A repository, model, requirements layer, evidence package, or governance mechanism may preserve, narrow, test, bind, reject, return, or carry purpose after it has been intentionally introduced.

It must not silently originate consequential purpose merely because a technical continuation is available.

When legitimate purpose is absent, the correct behavior is not architectural invention. The correct behavior is to identify the exact unanswered question and return it to the accountable source able to answer it.

This principle does not imply that one named human must answer every future question. It preserves the narrower architecture rule that consequential purpose must come from an accountable source appropriate to the boundary rather than from unsupported system inference.
