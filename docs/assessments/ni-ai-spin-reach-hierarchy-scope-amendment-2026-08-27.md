## Scope definition

The NI-AI Spin–Reach Hierarchy is a bounded system-scope model for preserving continuity while preventing capability, distribution, or channel expansion from becoming authority expansion.

```text
SCOPE
-> defines the legitimate field of operation
-> bounds SPIN
-> bounds REACH
-> preserves HIERARCHY
-> requires RETURN / REASSESSMENT when the boundary is reached
```

**Scope** is not merely a list of features. It defines what system relationships, channels, actors, interfaces, and transitions are presently inside the legitimate boundary and what remains outside it.

## Spin

**Spin** describes organization, circulation, recombination, or continued correspondence within the bounded system state. It may include meaning, context, media, workflow state, evidence, interaction, or other system relationships.

```text
SPIN != AUTHORITY
SPIN != EXECUTION PERMISSION
MORE INTERNAL CAPABILITY != LARGER SCOPE
```

Spin may increase coherence or make relationships more legible without changing who or what holds authority.

## Reach

**Reach** describes how bounded content, interaction, or system capability extends through legitimate channels and interfaces.

Reach may include:

- film;
- music video;
- audio;
- **podcast / hosted audio**;
- radio-style programming;
- live performance;
- broadcast / scheduled programming;
- social interaction;
- creator/audience exchange;
- device and interface surfaces;
- network and fiber delivery infrastructure.

Podcast is therefore explicitly inside the media-continuity reach scope as an audio / hosted-programming channel. Its inclusion does not collapse podcast into film, social media, radio, or another format; each channel retains its distinct form while participating in a shared continuity surface.

```text
GREATER REACH != GREATER AUTHORITY
MORE CHANNELS != MORE PERMISSION
FASTER DELIVERY != BROADER SCOPE
CROSS-MEDIA CONTINUITY != FORMAT ERASURE
```

## Hierarchy

**Hierarchy** preserves ordered responsibility and authority across the system. It is not a claim that one medium, interface, person, institution, or component is inherently more valuable than another.

The bounded hierarchy is:

```text
REALITY / HUMAN PURPOSE
-> ACCOUNTABLE SOURCE
-> GOVERNED SYSTEM SCOPE
-> BOUNDED RESPONSIBILITIES
-> INTERFACE / DEVICE
-> CHANNEL / MEDIA FORMAT
-> INTERACTION
-> RESULT / EVIDENCE RETURN
-> REASSESSMENT
```

A lower or later layer may carry, transform, present, or return information, but it does not inherit the authority of the source merely because the information reached it.

## Spin–Reach relationship

Spin and Reach correspond but do not authorize one another.

```text
SPIN
= ORGANIZE / PRESERVE / RELATE WITHIN SCOPE

REACH
= EXTEND / PRESENT / DISTRIBUTE THROUGH ALLOWED CHANNELS

HIERARCHY
= PRESERVE SOURCE, RESPONSIBILITY, PERMISSION, AND RETURN ORDER

SCOPE
= BOUND ALL THREE
```

Therefore:

```text
SPIN -> REACH
ONLY WITHIN SCOPE

REACH -> NEW CONTEXT
DOES NOT CREATE NEW AUTHORITY

NEW CONTEXT -> RETURN / REASSESS
BEFORE CONSEQUENTIAL CONTINUATION
```

## Entertainment continuity scope

Within the presently documented media-continuity interpretation, the system may conceptually preserve correspondence across:

```text
FILM
MUSIC VIDEO
AUDIO
PODCAST / HOSTED AUDIO
RADIO-STYLE PROGRAMMING
LIVE / BROADCAST
SOCIAL INTERACTION
```

These are distinct channels inside a shared governed continuity surface. Old-school and new-school entertainment may coexist: cinema, albums, scheduled programming, radio, hosted shows, live performance, and premieres may remain legitimate while interactive participation, social conversation, creator/audience exchange, remixing, community, and personalized interfaces extend the experience around them.

The delivery mechanism does not become the purpose, and the channel does not become the authority.

## Child / caregiver boundary

Where a social or entertainment interface is intended for children or caregiver-supported use, reach remains subordinate to explicit rules for identity, contact, recommendation, moderation, privacy, data handling, age-appropriate interaction, caregiver responsibility, safe exit, and human return.

No increase in media richness, network speed, personalization, or social reach may silently expand the system's authority over a child, caregiver, family, institution, or community.

## Frequency terminology

Within this scope, **frequency** may continue to be used as conceptual shorthand for continuity of signal, meaning, rhythm, or experience across system channels. Unless an actual measurable waveform, spectrum, carrier, acoustic signal, or communications-frequency parameter is being analyzed, the term does not constitute a physical-frequency claim.
