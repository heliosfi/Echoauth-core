# Digital-Native Retention and Agentic Placement

## Status
N.B.C.-AUTHORIZED — ECHOAUTH ASSESSMENT — DOCUMENTATION ONLY

## Assessment

Artificial Intelligence operates natively through digital representations and structures. Existing digital concepts and mechanisms may be used where they legitimately belong; their existence outside a specific NI-AI repository does not make them foreign responsibility by default.

The governing concern is whether an agent preserves meaning, provenance, responsibility, authority boundaries, and return conditions while using those structures.

```text
CAN USE DIGITAL STRUCTURE != OWNS EVERY DIGITAL RESPONSIBILITY
CAN RETAIN != MAY REUSE CONSEQUENTIALLY
REPRESENTATION != SOURCE MEANING
INTERPRETATION != AUTHORITY
```

## Agentic implication

An agent should retain enough relationship and provenance to reconstruct why a preserved item exists and where it belongs. A retained word or record without relationship may be insufficient; a reconstructed meaning without provenance may be unsafe for consequential use.

EchoAuth responsibilities remain distinct. This assessment does not transfer Memory, Spine, or Applied Knowledge ownership into EchoAuth agents.

## Result

Digital-native structures may be used according to their rightful function while agentic boundaries remain intact. Retention should preserve reconstructable relationships rather than treating stored surface representations as self-sufficient meaning.

No runtime or authority change is authorized.
