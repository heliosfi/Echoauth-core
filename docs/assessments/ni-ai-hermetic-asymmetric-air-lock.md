# NI AI Hermetic–Asymmetric Air-Lock

## Status

Founder-directed preservation record.

Documentation-only.

This document preserves an assessed engineering relationship within NI AI. It does not authorize runtime, implementation, deployment, execution, trading, funding movement, autonomous action, command execution, or capability expansion.

## Governing understanding

NI AI is built with governance.

Within that governed system, asymmetric and hermetic responsibilities are complementary.

They are not competing methods, interchangeable phases, or separate systems.

They preserve different conditions required for legitimate movement.

## Asymmetric responsibility

The asymmetric responsibility reduces ambiguity until bounded work becomes legitimate.

It:

- discovers relationships;
- narrows reality;
- identifies natural belonging;
- establishes category and specificity;
- connects evidence;
- identifies authoritative requirements;
- preserves governing meaning;
- prepares exact downstream responsibility.

Asymmetric work is complete only when downstream no longer needs to invent the meaning, scope, identity, or destination of the work it receives.

## Hermetic responsibility

The hermetic responsibility accepts only sufficiently bounded work.

It:

- preserves exact scope;
- performs only its explicit responsibility;
- protects local identity and integrity;
- refuses unauthorized expansion;
- returns a bounded result;
- stops after completion.

Hermetic work does not reinterpret upstream meaning or determine the broader architectural consequence of its result.

## Air-lock relationship

The transition between asymmetric and hermetic responsibility is an air lock.

The system must not cross from asymmetric preparation into hermetic completion while material ambiguity remains.

The system must not reconnect upstream until hermetic work has returned its bounded result through workflow.

The air lock therefore preserves two conditions:

1. downstream receives work that is specific enough to complete without invention;
2. upstream receives a result that is bounded enough to reconnect without distortion.

## Workflow relationship

Workflow carries the transition between asymmetric and hermetic responsibilities.

Workflow does not perform either responsibility's work.

It preserves:

- dispatch readiness;
- exact bounded assignment;
- authority and permission state;
- handoff;
- execution-crossing state;
- completion return;
- consumption and stop posture;
- next-transition availability.

## CEG relationship

The Crossroad Execution Gate preserves the structural crossing from valid authorization into one bounded execution cycle.

CEG does not create upstream meaning, authorization, or downstream completion.

It protects the air-lock crossing by enforcing the exact conditions under which authorized work may enter bounded execution, including proceed, refuse, hold, halt, and revoke outcomes.

## Governing law

> **Asymmetric prepares. Workflow carries. CEG protects the execution crossing. Hermetic completes and stops.**

Governance preserves the legitimacy of every transition without replacing any responsibility.

## Movement rule

The system does not move because a plausible continuation exists.

It moves only when preserved continuity establishes:

- which responsibility owns the work;
- what specific bounded work must move;
- which requirements govern it;
- whether authority and permission exist;
- where the work is going;
- and what state must return.

## Preservation rule

Do not dispatch hermetic work to compensate for unfinished asymmetric work.

Do not let hermetic completion expand its own scope.

Do not let workflow redefine meaning or perform completion.

Do not let CEG become authority, interpretation, or downstream execution.

Do not reconnect upstream before bounded completion has returned.

## Conclusion

The hermetic–asymmetric relationship is a governed air lock within one NI AI operating system.

Asymmetric responsibility makes exact bounded work possible.

Hermetic responsibility preserves that exactness through completion.

Workflow carries the relationship.

CEG protects the authorized execution crossing.

Governance ensures the movement remains legitimate.
