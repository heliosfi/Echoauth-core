## Where they touch

**1. No implied authority.** The DDR sovereignty amendment holds that
influence never silently converts into authority over the person. The NI-AI
thesis holds that capability, understanding, planning, permission, execution,
return, and memory never silently convert into authority at a system
boundary. Same discipline, two domains: the person and the system.

**2. Correspondence without inheritance.** The Technique requires source,
meaning, authority, evidence, and return to stay in correspondence across a
crossing. NI-AI requires understanding, passage, permission, execution, and
reassessment to correspond without inheriting one another's authority. In
both, a crossing is legitimate only while the relationships hold.

**3. Attribution stays with the source.** DDR: the AI assisted with
drafting and originated nothing; N.B.C. is the authority on whether an
interpretation corresponds. NI-AI: source-first interpretation, human-supplied
context distinguished from inference, interpretive capability ≠ interpretive
authority.

**4. Movement is earned at each boundary.** DDR: the next movement is earned;
"wait your turn"; return closes the lane. NI-AI: return ≠ reauthorization;
every consequential boundary requires fresh, independently validated
permission. Neither thesis permits coasting on a prior crossing.

**5. No-claim discipline.** DDR: no claim beyond the demonstration; the Salo
way is not the only way. NI-AI: evidence-bounded claims; absence is not
PASS; historical records stay historical. Both refuse to promote
documentation into proof.

**6. Words as carriers, not owners.** DDR: words are transformative carriers
of applied intelligence — they move understanding without becoming it.
NI-AI: words may make a relationship legible — legibility ≠ origin, naming ≠
ownership. Both treat language as a carrier surface governed by fidelity,
not as a source of authority.
