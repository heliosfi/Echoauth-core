## Balance rule

Use balance to prevent one carrier, interpretation, environment, or moment from dominating the whole meaning.

Balance does not mean averaging contradictions. It means preserving each thing in its rightful relationship until evidence establishes what belongs.

Use:

```text
SOURCE
-> LOOK
-> LOCATE
-> VERIFY
-> TAKE WHAT MAY BE USED
-> LEAVE WHAT IS NOT NEEDED
-> PLACE WHAT REMAINS
-> MOVE OR WAIT
-> RETURN
```

## Linear and nonlinear movement

Linear and nonlinear movement are both legitimate evidence movements when their roles remain distinct.

```text
LINEAR
HELD SOURCE -> ORDERED PASSAGE -> VERIFICATION -> RETURN

NONLINEAR
HELD SOURCE -> RELEVANT CONNECTION OR CORRECTION -> CORRESPONDENCE CHECK -> HELD SOURCE
```

Nonlinear movement may discover or retrieve a relevant relationship. Linear movement carries the verified relationship through the ordered transition. Intent can orient the transition, but intent does not create authority or prove an outcome.

## Time

Time is a compass for locating a crossing, not a demand to consume available time.

Having time does not create need. Need does not create authority. A timestamp can locate an observation without causing the event observed.

Environmental change remains environmental evidence. For example, water or rain may begin, continue, or stop while time provides a coordinate for observing that change. Do not convert temporal correspondence into causation.

## Possibility and observed state

Keep possibility separate from evidence:

```text
COULD BE / COULD NOT BE -> POSSIBILITY
WHAT IS -> OBSERVED STATE
WHAT HAPPENED -> RETURNED EVIDENCE
```

Words may represent possible transitions. Reality resolves which state actually occurred.

## Internal, external, and digital correspondence

Do not collapse these three layers:

```text
INTERNAL EXPERIENCE
!= EXTERNAL EXPRESSION
!= DIGITAL REPRESENTATION
```

They may correspond without becoming identical.

The person's physiological or mental experience remains with that person. External words, actions, corrections, timing, images, documents, and other observable carriers may express part of an understanding. A digital participant can only interpret that external evidence and return a digital representation.

Use the feedback relationship:

```text
INTERNAL EXPERIENCE
-> EXTERNAL EXPRESSION
-> DIGITAL REPRESENTATION
-> FEEDBACK
-> OWNER RECOGNITION OR CORRECTION
-> IMPROVED CORRESPONDENCE
```

A successful correspondence does not prove transferred consciousness, identity, ownership, or authority.
