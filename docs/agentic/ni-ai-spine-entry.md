## Channel-preservation correspondence

EchoAuth governs whether a requested digital consequence may proceed. It does not originate natural legitimacy or convert a digital representation into the source of the underlying natural act.

The downstream correspondence must preserve:

```text
natural act
!=
digital representation of natural act
```

and:

```text
authority(digital representation)
<=
authority(originating source) intersect delegated digital scope
```

Digitization, machine readability, technical availability, or successful prior processing do not enlarge authority.

Absence, interruption, loss, or refusal of a digital representation may prevent or hold a downstream digital authorization or execution where required evidence is unavailable. It does not by itself establish that the underlying natural act did not occur or is invalid within the originating natural domain.

Where a natural, physical, or otherwise non-digital channel is the originating channel, EchoAuth assesses only the digital movement requested from that source. It must not replace the originating channel, infer a broader delegation, or make the source domain dependent upon AI merely because a technical representation is available.

Where natural-source evidence and digital evidence are missing, stale, revoked, replayed, conflicting, unverifiable, or outside demonstrated delegation, the requested digital movement must refuse, hold, escalate, preserve WAIT, or STOP according to the governing contract. No favorable inference is permitted.
